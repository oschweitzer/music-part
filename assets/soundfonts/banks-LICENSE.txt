Sample banks (recordings of one instrument each), from the FreePats project
(https://freepats.zenvoid.org/). All three are published under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0
public domain dedication (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): no permission,
credit or fee is needed to use, change or distribute them.

nylon-guitar.sf2     Spanish classical guitar, version 2019-06-18. Recorded from a Spanish
                     classical guitar and created by roberto@zenvoid.org for FreePats.
electric-guitar.sf2  FSBS Electric Guitar Clean #1, bridge pickups, small size, version
                     2026-08-07. Direct sampling of a Fender electric guitar, processed through an
                     amplifier and effects rack. From the FreePats project.
electric-bass.sf2    Finger Bass YR, version 2019-09-30. Samples created by Andrea Biasior from a
                     Yamaha RBX bass guitar, with small edits by roberto@zenvoid.org.

The files are downloaded by `pnpm fetch:soundfont` with a pinned checksum and are not in git.
